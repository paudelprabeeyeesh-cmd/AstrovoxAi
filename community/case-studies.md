## Enterprise AI Deployment

**Company**: TechCorp Global
**Industry**: Technology
**Size**: 10,000+ employees
**Use Case**: Internal knowledge base and developer productivity

### Challenge
TechCorp needed a unified AI assistant for their engineering team that could access internal documentation and provide accurate, context-aware responses.

### Solution
Deployed Astrovox AI with custom knowledge base integration, enabling engineers to query documentation, generate code, and get instant answers to technical questions.

### Results
- 40% reduction in time spent searching documentation
- 25% increase in developer productivity
- 90% satisfaction rate among users
- 99.9% uptime over 6 months
