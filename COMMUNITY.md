# Community Guidelines

## Our Values

AstrovoxAI is built on open collaboration, technical excellence, and user privacy. We believe in:

- **Openness**: Transparency in our roadmap, code, and decision-making
- **Inclusion**: Welcoming contributors and users of all backgrounds
- **Technical Excellence**: High standards for code quality, documentation, and design
- **User Privacy**: Protecting user data and respecting privacy by design
- **Responsibility**: Building AI systems that are safe, fair, and accountable

## Code of Conduct

All community members are expected to adhere to our [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Key principles:
- Be respectful and constructive
- No harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- No spamming or self-promotion
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
