# Onboarding Call Script

Duration: 15 minutes
Goal: Understand their workflow, demo the product, identify confusion points

## Timeline

### 2 min: Their Problem
- "Walk me through your typical day as an SDR."
- "What's the most painful part of your outreach workflow?"
- "How much time do you spend on research vs. actually sending emails?"
- "What tools are you using now? What's missing?"

### 4 min: Demo
- Show the pill-shaped composer
- Demonstrate generating a personalized outreach sequence
- Show the campaign dashboard
- Highlight the AI research assistant

### 6 min: Watch Them Use It
- Ask them to create a campaign for a real prospect
- Watch for confusion points (note every "wait, how do I..." moment)
- Ask them to rate the generated content (1-5)
- Note: every confusion is a bug

### 3 min: Price Reaction
- "We're testing at $29/month for unlimited access."
- "Would that fit your budget?"
- "What would make this a no-brainer purchase?"
- "Who else on your team would use this?"

## Post-Call Actions

1. Log all confusion points to USERS.md
2. Update onboarding flow based on feedback
3. If they say yes to pricing: send Stripe test link
4. If they say no: note the blocker

## Success Criteria

- User can create a campaign in <3 min without help
- User rates generated content >= 4/5
- User says "I'd pay $29 for this" or gives specific feedback
