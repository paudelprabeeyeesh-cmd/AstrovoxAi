Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Last updated: 2026-09-14

1. Definitions

"Controller" means the entity determining the purposes and means of processing personal data. "Processor" means AstrovoxAI processing data on behalf of the Controller.

2. Processing Details

AstrovoxAI processes personal data as a processor on behalf of customers who are controllers. Processing includes storing user interactions, AI-generated responses, and usage analytics.

3. Security Measures

We implement encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and regular security audits to protect processed data.

4. Sub-processors

We use third-party sub-processors including cloud hosting providers and AI model providers. A current list is available upon request.

5. Data Subject Rights

We assist controllers in fulfilling data subject requests for access, rectification, erasure, and portability.

6. Contact

For DPA inquiries, email dpo@astrovox.ai
