# Hiring Playbook

## Engineering
- Senior Backend: $150-200K + equity
- Senior Frontend: $150-200K + equity
- ML Engineer: $180-250K + equity
- DevOps: $150-200K + equity

## Sales
- SDR: $60-80K + commission
- Account Executive: $120-180K + commission
- CSM: $80-120K + commission

## Process
1. Post on LinkedIn, Y Combinator Work at a Startup
2. Screen: 15 min call
3. Tech screen: 1 hour coding
4. Onsite: 4 hours system design + culture
5. Offer: 48 hours

## Interview Rubric
- Technical: Can they ship?
- Culture: Do they care about users?
- Grit: Have they built things before?
