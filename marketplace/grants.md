# Grants

Astrovox offers grants up to $50,000 for open-source projects that integrate with our platform.

## Eligibility

- Open-source project (MIT, Apache, or GPL license)
- Integrates with Astrovox AI API or SDKs
- Benefits the developer community
- Active development and maintenance

## Grant Types

### Innovation Grant ($10,000 - $25,000)
For novel integrations and tools.

### Community Grant ($5,000 - $10,000)
For community-focused projects.

### Research Grant ($25,000 - $50,000)
For academic or research projects.

## Application Process

1. Submit proposal via grants.astrovox.ai
2. Review by grants committee (2 weeks)
3. Interview with team (30 min)
4. Decision within 4 weeks

## Proposals Should Include

- Project description and goals
- Technical approach
- Timeline and milestones
- Budget breakdown
- Team background
- Community impact
