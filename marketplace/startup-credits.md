# Startup Credits

Astrovox offers up to $100,000 in API credits for startups.

## Eligibility

- Founded within the last 2 years
- Less than $1M in funding
- Building a product that uses AI
- Not previously received credits

## Credit Tiers

### Seed ($10,000)
For early-stage startups
- $10,000 in API credits
- 6 months validity
- Basic support

### Series A ($50,000)
For growth-stage startups
- $50,000 in API credits
- 12 months validity
- Priority support
- Co-marketing opportunities

### Accelerator ($100,000)
For select high-potential startups
- $100,000 in API credits
- 18 months validity
- Dedicated support
- Investor introductions
- Office hours with team

## How to Apply

1. Apply at startups.astrovox.ai
2. Fill out application form
3. Submit pitch deck
4. 15-min interview
5. Decision within 2 weeks

## Requirements

- Monthly usage reporting
- Logo placement on website
- Case study after 6 months
- Feedback on product
