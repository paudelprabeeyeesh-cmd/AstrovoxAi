# ADR-011: Tape-Based Reverse-Mode Autograd

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Training requires gradients of scalar losses w.r.t. tensor parameters. Forward-mode AD is inefficient for the typical case of many inputs and few outputs.

## Decision
`autograd` records every differentiable op into a `Tape` of `Variable`s (value, gradient, creator). `Tape.Backward(seed)` walks the tape in reverse, accumulating gradients via the chain rule; intermediates use their accumulated gradient as the seed. Nil seed defaults to ones.

## Consequences
- Reverse-mode gives O(1) backward passes for scalar losses.
- The tape is append-only per step; a new forward pass starts a fresh tape.
- Ops (`Add`, `Mul`, `Neg`, ...) define local gradients and register themselves.
