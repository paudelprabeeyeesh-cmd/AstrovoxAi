# Problem

B2B SaaS sales development representatives (SDRs) at companies with 5-50 employees waste 15+ hours per week on manual research, personalization, and follow-up sequencing. Existing tools are either too generic (no niche context) or too expensive ($500+/month) for small teams.

AstrovoxAI solves this by providing an AI-native workspace that understands SaaS sales context, automates outreach personalization, and manages campaign sequences in one place.
